Business analysis for AI

Most of the value in a technology rollout gets decided before a single model is built — in whether the team is solving a problem worth solving. I start with the business and the end user: what are they trying to achieve, where does the workflow break down, and what would make the product genuinely better? From there, I translate the need into the right capability — AI, automation, analytics, or a better data product — and measure whether it creates real business value. Progress is only progress when the business and the people using the product can feel the difference.

02

Find the bottleneck

03

04

Prove the return

Pilot with one team, measure hours or errors saved, then make the case for scaling it.
